Privacy Policy

Atlis · Effective October 11, 2026

Overview

Atlis is an educational investing app operated by Zachary Zaslavsky ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Atlis handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Atlis has no user accounts and no login. Most of your information stays on your own device. We do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information you provide

When you set up and use the app, you may provide:

  • Your first name, used only to greet you in the app.
  • Your investor profile answers (your goal, experience level, risk tolerance, and investing style).
  • Your watchlist (the stock symbols you choose to follow) and any price alerts you create.
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one — it stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Questions you type to the AI copilot.
  • These are stored on your device. Some are sent to the services below only when needed to provide a feature you use (for example, your question is sent to our AI provider to generate an answer).

Brokerage information (only if you connect one)

Connecting a brokerage is optional. If you choose to connect one, the link is handled by Plaid Inc. You enter your brokerage username and password directly with Plaid — Atlis never sees or stores your brokerage login.

After a successful connection, Atlis receives read-only information about your holdings and balances so it can show your portfolio. Atlis cannot place trades or move money. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored connection.

Information collected automatically

Atlis does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking software. When the app contacts our backend to fetch live data, our server may process technical information such as your device's IP address and the request being made, for security and to operate the service. We do not build advertising profiles from this.

How your information is used

We use the information described above only to make the app work, including to:

  • Show live prices, charts, market news, and company information for the symbols you look at.
  • Generate AI answers, debates, and starter ideas based on your question and profile.
  • Show alerts for the stocks you track and the price targets you set.
  • Display your real holdings, if you connect a brokerage.

Services we share information with

The app talks to our Atlis backend, which relays requests to the trusted services below so features can work. We share only what each service needs, and we do not sell your information to anyone. Each service has its own privacy policy:

  • Finnhub — market quotes, search, company fundamentals, and news (receives stock symbols). finnhub.io/privacy-policy
  • Yahoo Finance — index levels and historical price charts (receives stock symbols).
  • StockTwits — social "buzz" / sentiment (receives no personal information).
  • Anthropic (Claude) — powers the AI copilot, debates, and starter ideas (receives the question you type and your investor-profile answers). anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Plaid — brokerage connection, only if you connect one (handles your brokerage login directly). plaid.com/legal

Early-access list (website)

If you join the Atlis early-access list on our website, we collect only the email address you enter. We use it to tell you when Atlis launches and about major updates, never for anything else, and we never sell it. The list is run by our email provider (Kit, kit.com/privacy), which stores the address for us. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

About the AI

When you ask the AI copilot a question, the text you type and your investor-profile answers are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. Do not type sensitive personal information (such as account numbers, passwords, or government IDs) into the chat.

Your choices and control

You are in control of your information:

  • Edit or remove your name and watchlist at any time in the app.
  • Disconnect a connected brokerage at any time, which removes the stored connection.
  • Use "Start over" in Settings to clear your profile and saved data from the device.
  • Delete the app to remove the information stored on your device.

Children

Atlis is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is rated accordingly. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us at zacharyzaslavsky11@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" above. Significant changes will be reflected in the app.
